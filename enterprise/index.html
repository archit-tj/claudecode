Methodology & assumptions

  • VMware: billable cores = hosts × CPUs × max(cores per CPU, 16). Broadcom sells per-core subscriptions with a 16-core minimum per CPU. Reported list prices: VCF ~$350/core/yr, VVF ~$135/core/yr. Negotiated deals commonly land 30–55% below list. Check your actual quote, since price lists and minimum-order rules vary by region and contract.
  • VMware exit: one-time migration (per-VM services) plus retraining and parallel running, then the alternative platform's annual cost (set as % of the VMware quote). Break-even = one-time cost ÷ annual saving.
  • Cloud: savings levers apply sequentially to avoid double counting: rightsizing (~15% of compute if not done), non-prod scheduling (~65% of non-prod hours saved), commitments on uncovered steady compute (~28% saving, typical 1-year Savings Plan/CUD range is 20–40%), storage clean-up (~10% of non-compute). Total capped at 45%. Flexera reports 27–32% of cloud spend is wasted on average.
  • Laptops: TCO = purchase + support + software + downtime (hours × employee cost) − resale. Industry studies put purchase price at roughly 25–50% of lifecycle cost.
  • Estimates are indicative and not a quote. Prices in INR; USD conversion uses the rate you enter.
